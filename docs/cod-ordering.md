# COD ordering and owner-only vouchers

Approved October 25, 2026 by the repository operator/user, including explicit implementation authorization. This contract supersedes new-order payment selection in DEC-06 and platform-voucher issuance/redemption in the checkout extension. It does not authorize retained-data or remote deployment.

## Operating behavior

New orders pay on delivery. Cash or bank transfer is arranged externally. Restaurants self-deliver or hire their own deliverer; external deliverers advance the restaurant's payment and collect from the customer. No shipper accounts, advance ledger, bank proof or customer-collection confirmation is introduced. Restaurant fulfillment remains pending → accepted → delivering → completed, with the existing food-ready milestone, pending-only cancellation/rejection and terminal guards. Failed delivery/refusal/advance disputes remain external; completion does not prove collection.

Fixed verified points, surveyed fees, recipient privacy, accepted exact money snapshots, quote conflicts and durable idempotency remain unchanged. Zero totals need no payment. Admin retains account/restaurant/support administration and manual commission reconciliation, without new customer-payment duties.

## API and persistence

Preview and fresh create require `payment_method: "cod"`; `accepted_qr_version` is not accepted for COD. Nonzero COD payment is `not_tracked`; zero COD is `not_required`. Payment confirmation returns409 `PAYMENT_NOT_TRACKED` for COD. Filters/projections accept the new method/status. No paid fact is generated by completion.

Create continues parsing legacy cash/QR payloads solely to preserve their original canonical hashes and committed keyed replays. Under the existing actor/account lock, successful prior requests are recovered before rejecting an uncommitted legacy intent with422 `PAYMENT_METHOD_RETIRED`. Unknown results retain their original payload/key; a definitive rejection allows a new COD intent. Never silently rewrite an old intent or money acceptance.

QR proposal/review and new QR image uploads return410 `QR_RETIRED`; authorized committed config replays remain readable. Historical settings, suspension for safety, protected images and order-linked receipt/refund/dispute operations remain available. Public payment options advertise COD only. Historical instruction snapshots remain usable under existing suspension/terminal/paid guards.

Forward migration `20261025010000_cod_ordering` expands payment/history enums and adds COD consistency checks. It updates no business rows. Runtime startup requires its completed marker; startup never migrates. Preserve prior migration checksums, facts and unknown payment history. Rehearse empty/full-chain and pre-cutover upgrade on disposable MySQL9.2.0 only; retained adoption requires separate operator workflow.

## Voucher and commission behavior

Only current restaurant owners create restaurant-funded vouchers. Admin voucher reads remain historical; fresh Admin create/toggle returns410 `PLATFORM_VOUCHERS_RETIRED`. Successful keyed replays remain recoverable under current authority. New preview/create rejects platform-funded and null-restaurant codes as `VOUCHER_UNAVAILABLE`, irrespective of active flags. Never silently remove an invalid code. No platform-voucher flags or old order snapshots are rewritten.

Keep current owner voucher syntax, immutable financial parameters, active-version controls, exact discount rounding, limits, no stacking/delivery discount and no quota restoration after cancel/reject. Commission = pre-discount dish subtotal × accepted rate, half-up once per completed order, excluding delivery. New COD orders create no platform subsidy. Existing platform-voucher orders retain their accepted discount and create their promised subsidy on completion, including after cutover. Signed historical finance remains reportable.
