# P06 — Guest cart and login continuity

Status: **accepted**, October 19, 2026. Reviewer: Codex self-review; not independent/human sign-off.

## Basis and delivered behavior

Prerequisite: [P05](P05.md) accepted before cart integration. HEAD `d7a6c31` plus V0.2 changes; final source fingerprint and review: [V0.2](V0.2.md). User explicitly requested this plan, including retaining non-sensitive dish intent on logout/account switch. No new policy/DEC needed.

- Public `/cart`, menu add/view-cart callbacks and quantity-aware buyer navigation; manager/Admin retain buyer cart access. Cart owns its state; app injects public catalog reads and composes features.
- Versioned allowlisted localStorage intent: restaurant/dish IDs/names, quantities and accepted unit prices only. Exact BigInt subtotal; maximum 99 per dish, 50 dish types, 200 total portions. Queued reducer actions preserve rapid additions. Corrupt/unknown/private fields are rejected; denied storage retains an in-memory draft with a reload warning.
- Cross-restaurant consent dialog supports safe initial focus, Tab/Shift+Tab loop, Escape/cancel, confirmation and focus restoration. Cancel preserves the original draft.
- Opening cart, login/identity change and tab focus refresh current catalog with cancellation/stale-result protection. Hidden/sold-out dishes remain marked in the draft; failed reads preserve quantities and retry. Closed/non-accepting restaurants cannot add dishes.
- Old/current prices shown; only explicit consent updates accepted prices. Same-dish additions do not silently accept a changed price. Fees remain unknown; subtotal is an estimate.
- `/cart` is a safe login return. Reload/login/logout/account change preserve public intent; auth focus checks clear private account views and preserve anonymous login forms. No recipient, token, order, uncertain-intent key or recovery payload is persisted by this feature. Checkout remains unavailable until P15/P16; login never submits an order.
